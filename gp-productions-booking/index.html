About

Magnolia Booking Co. is a Georgia-based booking operation placing independent artists on stages across Athens and Augusta. We work with a focused roster of emerging acts and build long-term relationships with the venues and promoters that matter in this region.

We're not a corporation. We're a music-first operation that moves fast, communicates straight, and gets things done without layers of bureaucracy between the artist and the stage.

Grayson Pope Head of Booking — Magnolia Booking Co.
