The work

A slower, steadier kind of therapy.

No quick fixes or scripted exercises. We move at the pace your nervous system can actually hold.

01 — Listen

Make room first

Before we change anything, we make space to hear what's actually going on — underneath the story you've been telling yourself.

02 — Locate

Find the pattern

Anxiety, burnout, the cycle that keeps repeating — there's usually a younger part of you trying to keep you safe. We get curious about it together.

03 — Loosen

Practice something new

Small, repeatable shifts you can carry into your week. Not homework — practice. The kind that actually changes how the next moment lands.

About

I'm Dr. Maren Reyes. Glad you found your way here.

I've spent the last fifteen years sitting with people in some of the hardest weeks of their lives — and watching them, slowly, find their footing again.

My clients are often high-functioning on the outside and quietly unraveling on the inside. Founders, parents, caretakers, creatives. People who are very good at holding it together and very tired of having to.

Together we slow down. We get curious. We make room for the parts of you that haven't had a turn to speak.

— Maren

FAQ

Common questions, answered gently.

I've never been to therapy before. Is that okay?
More than okay. A lot of my clients are first-timers. We'll go at a pace that feels manageable, and you'll never be asked to share more than you're ready to.
How long does therapy usually take?
It depends — some people come for a season around a specific transition, others stay longer to do deeper work. We'll check in regularly so you always know where you stand.
Do you take insurance?
I'm in-network with Aetna, Lyra, and Modern Health. For other plans, I provide monthly superbills you can submit for out-of-network reimbursement.
What if I don't know what to talk about?
That's a perfectly normal place to start. Part of my job is helping you find the thread. We'll figure out together what wants attention.
