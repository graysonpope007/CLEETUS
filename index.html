About

Magnolia Booking Co. is a music-first talent booking and development agency based in Augusta, Georgia. We book artists into the right rooms, help venues build stronger nights, and keep every show focused on growth.

We are direct, confident, and practical. Artists can trust us to have their best interest in mind. Venues can trust us to bring professional talent, clear communication, and a plan that helps the night make money and build reputation.

Artists Show strategy, venue placement, and development beyond one-off gigs.
Venues Reliable talent, clean logistics, and shows built around the room.
Promoters Fast communication, useful assets, and confident local execution.

How Booking Works

Clear Steps. Better Shows.

Whether you are a venue owner, concert promoter, band member, or artist, Magnolia keeps the process direct. We define the right opportunity, package the pitch, contact the room, and help move the show from idea to confirmed date.

01

Define the Fit

We look at the artist, venue, audience, timing, budget, and show format before we pitch anything.

02

Package the Pitch

We prepare clean artist info, music links, draw notes, promo assets, and a simple reason the show makes sense.

03

Book the Room

We contact the venue, handle follow-up, negotiate the details, and keep both sides moving toward a confirmed date.

04

Build the Night

We support promotion, logistics, and day-of communication so the artist and venue can trust the plan.
