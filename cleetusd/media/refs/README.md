# Reference pictures

One folder per brand, artist, project or look. Put a few pictures in it.

    refs/glm/…            Good Life Music
    refs/magnolia/…       Magnolia Booking
    refs/sky-ciela/…      a specific artist
    refs/warm-film/…      a look rather than a client

Cleetus lists these with `list_references` and starts a generation FROM one of
them, rather than from a paragraph describing it. That is the difference
between a Sky Ciela cover that looks like her last one and a new invention:
the exact blue of a brand survives a file and does not survive a sentence.

Anything a sampler can open counts — png, jpg, jpeg, webp. A PDF brand guide is
a perfectly reasonable thing to keep in here and will be listed as a file that
cannot be used as a reference, rather than silently ignored.

Nothing in here is picked automatically. Cleetus reports what exists and says
which picture it started from, so a result that looks like something has a
reason you can trace.

Not in git: these are your artwork.
