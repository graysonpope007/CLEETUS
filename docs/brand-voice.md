# Magnolia Booking Co. Brand Voice

## Position

Magnolia Booking Co. is a music-first talent booking and artist development agency. The public voice should feel like a larger professional agency without falling into corporate lingo.

## Audience

Write for venue owners, concert promoters, band members, and artists. The primary market is Augusta, with Athens and wider Georgia used as growth markets.

## Voice

- Direct and confident.
- Professional, but still personal.
- Music-first before business jargon.
- Clear about money, growth, trust, and execution.
- Protective of the artist's best interest while still being useful to venues.

## Core Message

Magnolia can book professional shows at venues, help the venue make money, and help artists and rooms build a name in Augusta and Georgia.

## Trust Promise

Artists, venues, and promoters can trust Magnolia to have their best interest in mind and to communicate clearly through the booking process.

## Use More

- "music-first"
- "professional talent agency"
- "artist development"
- "venue development"
- "book better shows"
- "build the night"
- "make the room work"
- "clear booking contact"
- "direct communication"
- "Augusta"

## Use Less

- corporate lingo
- vague creative language
- over-polished startup copy
- passive promises
- anything that makes Magnolia sound like a hobby project

## Public CTAs

- Book Talent
- Submit Your Music
- Book This Artist
- Tell Us What You Need Booked
- Email Magnolia
